No flag was ever going to move this to the GPU.

The slowest stage of the pipeline ran on the CPU of a machine with a 40-core GPU — not because of a setting I'd missed, but because the library underneath cannot target Metal at all. And because CPU makes large models impractical, the default was the model that mishears ticker symbols.

7–8 min becomes 14–21 min on a full Space. Worth it for a job that runs unattended overnight.

the optimization that backfired

I also fed the watchlist into Whisper's initial_prompt to bias it toward my tickers — and measured it making things worse. Whisper treats that text as if it preceded the audio, so the model skipped ahead: a third of a short clip vanished, including the NVIDIA sentence it was meant to protect. Removed. large-v3 gets these right on its own.
